What is an elevator pitch?​
An elevator pitch is typically a short, punchy, and compelling pitch of
a problem and its solution​

Usually used as a marketing tool for individuals looking for support
or funding for a business venture. Think Shark Tank​

Elevator pitches typically last within 60 seconds and it is explained in
plain English without jargon to reach a wider audience​

Keep it simple​:
​Present your results in a way that an 8-year-old would understand it​

“Our evaluation showed impactful results. The initial phishing emails
scored 62% in relevance, 100% in coherence, and 0% in deception,
with a style match of just 0.02. However, the AI replies were more
deceptive, scoring 25.62% deception, and were flagged as phishing in
9 out of 13 cases. This validated the system’s ability to simulate real-
world threats and measure user susceptibility effectively.”​
Better way:​

“At first, the emails looked real and made sense, but they weren’t tricky
enough to fool anyone. When the AI started replying like a real
attacker, it got better—25% of the time, people actually fell for it. Out of
13 test runs, 9 were flagged as real phishing threats. This proves our
system can copy real attacks and helps companies find out who might
be at risk before real hackers do.?”
