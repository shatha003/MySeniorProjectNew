We wanted to update you on our progress with the CHEA project. Since our last meeting, we have implemented 6 new AI-powered features that enhance the application's educational capabilities. Here is a brief overview:

1. **AI Scan Analysis** — When students scan a link or file, the AI now provides a plain-language explanation of the scan results, so younger students can actually understand what the numbers mean instead of just seeing "malicious" or "clean."

2. **AI Attack Narrative** — In the password checker, the AI writes a short story from a hacker's perspective explaining how they would try to crack the student's password. It never sends the actual password — only its characteristics like length and entropy.

3. **AI Phishing Generator** — The Phishing Dojo now has an AI Challenge mode that generates unique phishing emails on demand. This means students get unlimited practice material instead of memorizing the same 23 static emails.

4. **AI Scenario Simulator** — A new page where students work through 5 AI-generated cybersecurity scenarios (e.g., "You receive a suspicious email from your school... What should you do?"). Each session is unique.

5. **AI Security Posture Assessment** — A new page with 15 questions about real cybersecurity habits. After answering, the AI generates a personalized security report card with a grade, strengths, weaknesses, and actionable tips.

6. **Security Buddy** — A daily AI-generated challenge that appears on the Dashboard, giving students one concrete security task to try each day. It changes automatically every midnight.

All 6 features support both English and Arabic, and they all use the Grok-4-Fast model through the OpenRouter API. Each feature also awards XP and integrates with our gamification system.
